# AUI course overview
Type: support · Version: v2 (2026-10-07) · Source: AUI syllabus (Syl) pp. 2–4; Lesson 1 (L1) slides 24–42. Citations: "L1 s27" = Lesson 1, slide 27.

## Goal (L1 s27; Syl p2)
Hard and soft skills for ACCESSIBLE and INCLUSIVE INNOVATION: design methods and technologies for interactive solutions to complex user requirements, especially of fragile people and people with special needs.
- Technical areas: interactive IoT, VR/AR/MR, conversational agents, GenAI. Domains: health and well-being, education, sustainability.
- Technology is a means, not an end: it serves users' needs (L1 s24). Innovation = a replicable idea that satisfies a specific need (s28).

## Three subject areas (Syl p3; L1 s34)
1. Development cycle: requirements elicitation (needs/goal-oriented), design (scenario-based), prototyping (sketching).
2. "Beyond the desktop" paradigms and their technology: tangible, full-body in smart spaces, VR/AR/MR, conversational (LLMs).
3. Experimental HCI research: research questions, variables, between/within-group designs, qualitative/quantitative data, data gathering, analysis, reliability, validity.

## Paradigms and projects (L1 s35, s40–42)
- Paradigms: conversational; shared VR; full-body; tangible; XR tracking (head, eye, gesture); multimodal.
- Project areas: LLM (multiparty) conversation; GenAI content for education, therapy or daily life; tangible; full-body; emotional interaction; XR (maybe).
- A theme = user population × needs × context of use × technology.

## Learning outcomes (Syl pp2–3, Dublin Descriptors)
DdD1 knowledge; DdD2 applying it (elicit requirements → UX design → build → evaluate empirically); DdD3 informed judgement (risks, ethics); DdD4 communication (presentations, docs, video pitch); DdD5 autonomous learning.

## Approach (Syl pp3–4)
Project- and problem-based, on real cases with partner experts. Materials on WeBeep. Useful, not required: UX/web design, JS, C#, Unity, electronics. Books: Lazar et al., Research Methods in HCI (2017); Preece, Sharp, Rogers, Interaction Design (2015).
