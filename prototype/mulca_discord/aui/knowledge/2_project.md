# Project rules and deliverables
Type: normative · Version: v4 (2026-10-07) · Source: Lesson 1 (L1) slides 38–51; Lesson 2 (L2) slides 108–110; syllabus (Syl) p. 4

## Rules
- Topics come from the teaching team's catalogue; students form groups, which rank 3 preferences; the team assigns one, respecting the ranking where possible (L1 s43). Own proposals: in general no (L1 s51).
- At least one designer and one engineer per team; no designers-only (L1 s43). Solo: in general no; for major-force reasons (health, family), contact the teaching team (L1 s51). Exchange students plan their timing so as not to stress the group (L1 s43).
- Equal effort from all; all members attend every tutoring (tracked). Unbalanced participation → a meeting; at the end, members may get different scores (L1 s44).

## Activities and milestones
- Understand stakeholders' needs → state of the art → design, prototype, evaluate → document → present (L1 s39). Each tutoring has results due, per the schedule (L1 s45).
- Demo Madness: every group pitches (+ demo), early December, half day, mandatory; feedback from peers, teachers, tutors, external stakeholders (L1 s38, s46).
- Final presentation + delivery at an official exam date (dates TBA); any session, winter (Jan–Feb) STRONGLY RECOMMENDED. Erasmus/double-degree: present BEFORE going home (L1 s46).

## Deliverables on the exam date (L1 s47; L2 s108)
1 Project report (professional quality; must include the UNG model and scenarios, L2 s108); 2 presentation (ppt); 3 prototype with source code; 4 video pitch (max 2 min); 5 complementary material, if any. The syllabus also lists video scenarios, a user manual, and slides for the 20-min final presentation (Syl p4).
Empirical evaluation: optional in some projects, mandatory in others; at any stage, with representatives of the stakeholders; if done, its report is included (L1 s48–49).

## Report structure (L2 s109–110)
1 Cover + abstract · 2 Team · 3 Member contribution + table of contents (page numbers) · 4 Executive summary · 5 Requirements · 6 State of the art · 7 UX design · 8 Implementation (L2 s109) · 9 Empirical evaluation · 10 Value proposition · 11 Discussion, future work · 12 Business (optional) · Bibliography (ACM/IEEE) · Annexes (L2 s110).
Sections the tracked deliverables cover (others: ask the teaching team):
- §5: per the UNG model – stakeholders, needs, context, constraints, goals. Needs are properties of the users; goals are properties of the system, more concrete than needs, and say what, not how (L2 s109).
- §6: related research works, projects and market products (L2 s109).
- §7: general approach (max 1 page: paradigms, content, main tasks); user workflow diagrams; ALL relevant scenarios (sub-scenarios if needed); dialogue diagrams for conversational projects (L2 s109).
