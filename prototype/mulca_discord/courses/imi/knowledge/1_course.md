# IMI course overview
Type: support · Version: v1 (2026-10-14) · Source: IMI 2026/27 project-phase agenda, from the teaching team. Partial: the syllabus isn't in this material yet, so for anything beyond what's here (goals, grading, rules, people), refer students to the teaching team.

## What the course covers in the project phase
- Lectures on AI for coding (two parts), on how to write the project report (and a good thesis or scientific paper), and on accessibility principles.
- Project work in teams, with tutoring on Thursdays: teams review the state of the art related to their project (academic literature & market products), analyse user requirements (UNG model), design the UX with user scenarios, and assess technology feasibility and the architecture.
- Some weeks have no tutoring and are for autonomous project work; the schedule lists them.
